# Code of Conduct

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free, and inclusive collaboration
environment. Discuss ideas and evidence, assume good intent, protect private
and institutional data, and give actionable feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing
private information, or knowingly misrepresenting source institutions and
licenses. Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that
violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. They should
respond fairly, protect confidentiality as practical, and avoid conflicts of
interest.
