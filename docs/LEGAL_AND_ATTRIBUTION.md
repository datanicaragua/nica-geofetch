## INETER source

Implemented source: Instituto Nicaragüense de Estudios Territoriales (INETER),
2025 national hydrographic units adjusted to the Pfafstetter methodology,
levels 4-7, accessed through the official GeoServer WMS KML reflector.

Recommended provenance wording:

> Source: INETER, national hydrographic units adjusted to Pfafstetter, 2025.
> Retrieved from the official INETER GeoServer. Converted and validated with
> Nica-GeoFetch; the converted file is not represented as an official product.

## Known album restriction

The 2014 reference album's credits page reserves publication rights for the
participating institutions, prohibits commercial reproduction and use of its
content, and requires advance INETER permission for educational or divulgative
reproduction. The local MVP seed audit visually verified this statement on page
4 of the 36-page PDF.

The album statement is documented as known evidence; it is not silently
extended into a definitive license interpretation for the 2025 layers.
Institutional clarification is recommended before redistributing complete data
copies. This document is not legal advice.
