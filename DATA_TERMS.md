# Data terms

## Software versus data

The Apache License 2.0 applies to Nica-GeoFetch source code and synthetic test
fixtures. It does not grant rights in KML files, converted datasets, maps, or
other institutional content downloaded or processed with the software.

Nica-GeoFetch does not describe the INETER datasets as open data because an
explicit open-data license has not been identified. Source URLs, metadata,
checksums, validation summaries, and conversion tools may be published without
asserting a right to republish complete dataset copies.

## Known 2014 album statement

The credits page of the 2014 album *Cuencas Hidrográficas de Nicaragua bajo la
metodología Pfafstetter* states, in substance, that the participating
institutions reserve publication rights, commercial reproduction and use are
prohibited, and educational or divulgative reproduction requires prior INETER
permission.

That statement was visually verified in the local reference album during
MVP-1. It concerns the album and does not, by itself, establish a license for
the 2025 GeoServer layers. Seek written institutional clarification before
publicly redistributing complete data copies.

This is a factual project policy summary, not legal advice.

## Repository policy

Do not commit or publish real institutional KML files or converted outputs.
Synthetic fixtures are allowed. Generated audit summaries must avoid feature
coordinates and bulk attribute values.
